# My Favorite Series: *Breaking Bad*

![Breaking Bad Poster](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/6/61/Breaking_Bad_title_card.png)

---

## Overview
**Breaking Bad** is an American crime drama television series created by *Vince Gilligan*.  
It follows the transformation of **Walter White**, a high school chemistry teacher turned methamphetamine manufacturer.

> *“I am not in danger, Skyler. I am the danger.”* — Walter White

---

## Main Characters
1. **Walter White** — a chemistry teacher diagnosed with cancer who begins producing meth to secure his family’s future.  
2. **Jesse Pinkman** — Walter’s former student and partner in the drug trade.  
3. **Skyler White** — Walter’s wife, who struggles with his secret life.  
4. **Hank Schrader** — Walter’s brother-in-law and DEA agent.  
5. **Saul Goodman** — a colorful lawyer who helps keep Walter and Jesse out of trouble.

---

## What I Like About It
- The **character development** is incredible.  
- The **storytelling** is both intense and emotional.  
- The **cinematography** and **symbolism** make each episode a masterpiece.  
- It shows how **morality can blur** under pressure.
